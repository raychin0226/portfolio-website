----Junior Project Manager, becoming a Creative Director

Who I Am

I'm Raymond, a Junior Project Manager with a growing focus on creative and technical direction.

I've worked across AI exhibitions, immersive installations, VR hackathons, battle robotics, and AR product experiences — always sitting at the intersection of design, technology, and delivery.

I'm currently working toward becoming a Creative Project Director: leading teams that turn ambitious ideas into real, working experiences, not just polished decks.

Approach

I start by understanding the people and constraints behind a project — the stakeholders, the technical limits, and the real goal behind the brief.

From there, I coordinate the right people, keep communication clear between design and technical teams, and make sure execution stays aligned with the original vision.

My role isn't just tracking tasks — it's protecting the quality and clarity of the work as it moves from idea to delivery.

Philosophy

I believe good delivery is a creative act in itself — a great concept means nothing if the execution falls apart.

I care as much about process as I do about the final result, and I try to stay organized without losing sight of the bigger creative vision.

The best projects happen when structure and creativity work together, not against each other.
